Mayonnaise

Description

A recipe for a tasty, acidy mayo.

It's easily customizable, can be used for garlic, lemon, chilli, herb mayo, etc.

Ingredients

  • White wine vinegar - 27g
  • Dijon mustard - 18g
  • Salt - 8g
  • Egg yolk - 70g
  • Rape seed oil - 1L

Steps

  1. Mix everything together except oil.
  2. While mixing, add oil slowly.
  3. Add water for consistency if needed.
