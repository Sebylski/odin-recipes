Caramel

Description

A recipe for a dark, liquidy caramel.

Ingredients

  • Sugar - 1kg
  • Glucose - 400g
  • Whipping cream - 2-2.5L
  • Vanilla sugar - to flavor
  • Salt - to flavor (important!)
  • Some water for dissolving the sugar

Steps

  1. Cook sugar and glucose disolved in water until dark brown color.
  2. Add warmed up whipping cream.
  3. Flavour with salt and vanilla sugar.
