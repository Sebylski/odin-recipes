Caramel

Description

A recipe for a perfect brownie.

Ingredients for one 1/1 GN

  • Sugar - 1.35kg
  • Cocoa - 175g
  • Flour - 375g
  • Vanilla sugar - 75g
  • Butter - 0.5kg
  • Eggs - 10st
  • Salt - 15g
  • Dark choco chips - 125g

Steps

  1. Mix sugar and half of eggs on high speed.
  2. Add rest of eggs, low speed until ingredients combine well.
  3. Add mixed cocoa, flour, vanilla sugar and salt, mix slowly until combined.
  4. Add melted butter, mix.
  5. Add choco chips, spin slow until mixed.
  6. Bake at 170°C, fan 3/5, 35-40 minutes.
